# Compliance and inference boundaries

## Weak inference is allowed

Allow natural livestream reasoning when it does not create a new hard fact.

Prefer language such as:

- “从这些信息看……”
- “相对来说……”
- “你看这个状态……”
- “整体会给人一种……的感觉”
- “可以理解为……”

Avoid turning correlation into certainty with:

- “决定了”
- “必然”
- “一定”
- “绝对”
- “肯定”
- “百分百”
- “保证”

## Fact boundaries

Do not infer, unless verified product facts explicitly support it:

- age/sex → tenderness, taste, suitability, nutrition;
- activity → a specific meat-quality outcome;
- gross/net weight → loss reason or edible weight;
- fresh/raw → frozen/cold-chain status;
- fresh slaughter → guaranteed freshness at delivery;
- retained offal → claims about what competitors remove or why;
- tutorial/customer service → one-to-one culinary guidance or guaranteed cooking results.

## Advertising-risk language

Treat this as operational risk control, not legal advice.

Avoid product superlative/absolute/unique claims such as:

- 国家级、最高级、最佳;
- 全网第一、行业第一、唯一、顶级、极品、史上最好;
- 全网最低、最低价;
- 百分百、100%、绝对、保证、零风险、永久;
- 最牛、最强、最划算、无敌.

Do not mechanically ban every occurrence of “最”. Ordinary phrases such as “大家最关心的问题” can be acceptable when they do not claim the product is superior.

Do not fabricate or disparage competitors to strengthen the pitch.
