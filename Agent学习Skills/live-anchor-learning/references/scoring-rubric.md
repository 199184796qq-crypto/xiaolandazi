# Livestream anchor scoring rubric

Score 100 points total. User score remains authoritative; this rubric explains likely gaps.

| Dimension | Points | What good looks like |
|---|---:|---|
| 真人连续感 | 20 | Sounds like one person speaking continuously, not stitched modules or a report. |
| 商品讲深程度 | 20 | Keeps developing the product using verified material instead of retreating to parameters. |
| 节奏与承接 | 15 | Natural transitions, no abrupt stage change, no “回到正题/下一段”. |
| 口语自然度 | 10 | Spoken Chinese with varied fillers and sentence length; not copywriting prose. |
| 参数/链接克制 | 10 | Facts and links appear when useful but do not dominate the speech. |
| 重复控制 | 5 | Repeats key information with a new angle, not the same sentence. |
| 事实与合理推理 | 10 | Verified facts are correct; mild inference is hedged and not converted into certainty. |
| 广告合规 | 5 | Avoids product absolute/superlative claims, unsupported guarantees, false comparisons. |
| 收口自然度 | 5 | CTA emerges naturally from the product story instead of becoming customer-service boilerplate. |
